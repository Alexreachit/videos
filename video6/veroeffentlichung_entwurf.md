## Beschreibung
It is two in the morning and you tell yourself one more hour will not hurt. This video walks through what really happens to your body and brain when you skip sleep, hour by hour: the first 24 hours, microsleeps, hallucinations, the famous 11-day record, and what chronic short sleep does over the years. Plus simple habits that actually protect your sleep.

Not medical advice. Numbers are rough averages from public studies and differ from person to person.
