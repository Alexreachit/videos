Why you should never buy a festival ticket from an Instagram ad: in 2017, famous models promoted Fyre Festival, a "luxury" music festival on Great Exuma in the Bahamas. It promised luxury villas and gourmet meals – VIP packages with flights cost $12,000. But when guests arrived, they found disaster-relief tents and soaking-wet mattresses, and dinner was a cheese sandwich in a foam box. By the next morning, the festival was called off and guests were sent back to Miami. Its founder pleaded guilty to wire fraud and was sentenced to six years in prison in 2018. Would you have bought a ticket? 🎟
