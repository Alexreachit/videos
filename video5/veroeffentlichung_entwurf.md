## Beschreibung (Entwurf)
What does it really take to become a professional footballer? In this video you live through seven stages of a football career, from your first academy trial to your final whistle: the pay, the pressure, the injuries and the money decisions at every step.

All numbers are rough examples from public reports and studies. They differ by country, club and year. This video is for entertainment and general information only and is not financial or career advice.

Facts used (rough): fewer than 1 percent of boys who join an English academy before 14 become professionals (The Conversation, study of English academies); about 30 percent of scholars get a first professional contract (same study); average career about 8 years, most retire by 35 (Harrison Drury); Premier League average salary reported above 3 million pounds a year (theHRDIRECTOR, 2024); one former players group estimates around 60 percent face money trouble within 5 years of retiring (Xpro, via Harrison Drury).
