# TikTok 4: Why concert tickets cost so much (fees and resale)
The ticket says eighty dollars, so why does checkout say about one hundred?
Let's open the price tag.
First come the fees: a service fee, a facility fee, and an order fee, stacked on top.
On primary ticket sites, fees typically add roughly a quarter to the base price.
Resale sites often charge even more, averaging roughly thirty percent in fees.
But the biggest markup often happens before you even reach checkout.
Artists often price tickets below what fans would pay, to keep shows affordable and sold out.
That gap is an open invitation for resellers.
Professional brokers use software bots to grab large blocks of tickets within seconds of release.
Then they relist them, often at about double the face value.
Some sellers also use dynamic pricing, so the price climbs with demand in real time.
So much of what you pay above face value goes to platforms and resellers, not the artist.
New rules in the U.S. now require the full price to be shown up front.
Would you rather pay more up front, or fight the bots at exactly ten o'clock?
