# TikTok 3: Why "free" apps aren't free
You pay nothing for this app. So how does it make billions?
Every time you open it, a tiny auction runs behind the screen.
Advertisers bid to show you an ad, based on what the app knows about your interests.
The better the app knows you, the more an advertiser will pay for that one moment of your attention.
The highest bidder wins, the ad appears, and the app gets paid, often in about a tenth of a second.
A single ad view earns very little, often well under one cent.
But the more time you spend, the more auctions run, and the more money flows to the app.
Endless feeds, notifications and autoplay are no accident. Each one adds a few more seconds to your visit.
Multiply those seconds by millions of users, and a free app becomes a very profitable machine.
You are not really the customer here. Your attention is the product being sold.
So what is one hour of your attention worth to you?
