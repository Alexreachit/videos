# TikTok 3: Why "free" apps aren't free
You open a free app. You pay nothing. So how does the company earn billions?
Every time you open it, an auction runs in a fraction of a second.
Advertisers bid to show you something, based on what the app knows about your interests.
The more precisely the app knows you, the more an advertiser is willing to pay for that single moment of your attention.
The highest bidder wins, the ad appears, and the app gets paid, all before the screen loads.
The more time you spend, the more auctions happen, and the more money flows.
Endless feeds, notifications and autoplay are not accidents. Each one is designed to add a few more seconds to your visit.
Multiply a few seconds by millions of users, and a free app turns into a very profitable machine.
That is why these apps are built to keep you scrolling.
You are not the customer. Your attention is the product that gets sold.
So what is one hour of your attention worth to you?
