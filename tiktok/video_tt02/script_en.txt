# TikTok 2: Why airlines sell more seats than exist
An airplane has 180 seats. The airline sells maybe 190 tickets. Why would it do that?
Because some passengers simply never show up. Over thousands of flights, that happens every single day.
Business travelers change plans, connections run late, and some people just oversleep, so a small share of seats stays empty.
An empty seat earns nothing, but the plane still burns the same fuel.
So airlines sell a few extra tickets and bet that the empty seats will cancel out.
Most of the time the bet works, and nobody notices.
Airlines even track each route's no-show rate, so the number of extra tickets is a calculated guess, not a wild gamble.
Selling the same seat twice sounds unfair, but a plane that leaves with empty seats also pushes ticket prices up for everyone.
When it fails, the airline offers money to people who will take a later flight.
The offer goes up until someone says yes. That is cheaper for them than an angry full plane.
Agents start with a small voucher, and every few minutes the offer grows, until the price is high enough for someone to volunteer.
So next time, would you give up your seat for the right price?
